Region by region

Each region has its own α model, fitted on its own 2013–2021 overlap. The total is the sum of the regions. Errors are out of sample (each year predicted from a model fitted without it), shown for the model and, in grey, for a single constant ratio per month. The bias check compares each region with simulations of a correct model at that region's own noise level (pN = percentile of the real score; below 5 fails). "Low data" marks regions with too few fire months to validate tightly.

How to read it

Raw detections count every filtered hotspot. VIIRS pixels are about 1/7 the area of MODIS pixels, so one fire produces many more VIIRS dots, and the record jumps when VIIRS takes over.

Gridded counts each 0.05° cell (about 5.5 km) once per day. This removes the pixel-size effect. A smaller jump remains, because VIIRS also sees smaller and cooler fires.

Harmonized uses VIIRS as observed whenever it flew, and MODIS × α otherwise. α is learned from 2013–2021, when both sensors flew. It depends on the calendar month and on how active the region is that month, because VIIRS adds relatively fewer cells in a big fire month: both sensors already flag nearly every cell inside large fires. The result is in VIIRS-equivalent cell-days.
